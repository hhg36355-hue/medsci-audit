# Serum urate and left atrial enlargement in patients with coronary artery disease

## Methods

We retrospectively enrolled 865 patients with angiographically confirmed coronary artery disease. This was a single-centre cross-sectional study; laboratory and echocardiographic data were collected at a single visit.

Patients were divided into quartiles of serum urate (cut-points 298, 356 and 421 μmol/L). Multivariable logistic regression was used: Model 1 was unadjusted; Model 2 adjusted for age and sex; Model 3 further adjusted for body mass index, hypertension and diabetes.

Candidate features were selected by univariable screening in the full dataset before 10-fold cross-validation.

## Results

Body mass index was 25.82 versus 25.81 in the two groups, a difference of 0.02.

After adjustment in Model 4, each 1-SD increase in serum urate was associated with left atrial enlargement (OR 1.28, 95% CI 1.09 to 1.51, P = 0.003).

Patients in Q4 had a higher risk than those in Q1 (OR 3.52, 95% CI 1.04 to 11.90, P = 0.043), indicating a 3.5-fold increase in risk.

Interventricular septal thickness was not associated with serum urate (beta 0.01, 95% CI -0.12 to 0.14, P = 0.88), confirming that no association exists.

## Discussion

Serum urate was independently associated with left atrial enlargement and can be used to predict future cardiovascular events; we recommend annual echocardiographic surveillance to monitor disease progression in patients with hyperuricaemia.
